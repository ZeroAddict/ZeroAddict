## Primary Case Study: Researchetal (Ed-Tech Platform)

**Timeline:** 2024–2026 | **Role:** Sole PM, designer, developer | **Status:** Shipped (live April–May 2026)

### Problem
Academic researchers need credible, stable platforms. Young learners need clean, simple UX. Most ed-tech tools serve one but alienate the other.

### What I Built
- Full website with custom UI/UX
- "Flying book" animation (delight factor)
- Job category sections
- WhatsApp contact integration

### Key Product Decisions

| Decision | Why I Made It | What Happened |
|----------|---------------|----------------|
| WhatsApp instead of email form | Lower friction for Nigerian users | 3x more clicks than email CTAs |
| Clean geometric visual language | Signal credibility to researchers | Users stayed longer on page |
| Flying book animation | Add personality without hurting UX | Positive feedback from young learners |

### Evidence
- 📸 [Screenshots (PDF)](link)
- 💻 [GitHub Repo](https://ZeroAddict/Researchetal-landing)
- 🔗 Live link: researchetal.com (was live April–May 2026, hosting lapsed)
- 🎨 [Logo assets (light/dark)]()

### What I Learned
- WhatsApp CTA outperformed email by 3x → future projects: prioritize low-friction channels
- Hosting renewal isn't automatic → set calendar reminders
- Building alone means owning everything → next time, seek a collaborator

### Methodology Applied
This project followed my [4-stage product framework](link): Diagnose → Isolate Friction → Design → Unify & Align → Validate.

## Secondary Case Study: Egun Fishmongers (Makoko, Lagos)

**Timeline:** 2023 | **Role:** Product researcher | **Status:** Capstone proposal

### Problem
Senami Senayon is an Egun fishmonger. She wakes at 5 AM. She faces discrimination commuting to sell fish. Her children's education suffers. Government neglects her community.

### What I Did
- Desk research (McKinsey, Sahara Reporters, academic journals)
- User persona mapping
- Identified three intervention layers: digital marketplace, transport tracking, community education clinics

### Proposed Solution
- Centralized marketplace app with consumer hotspot prompts
- Route-tracked transport scheme
- Weekend basic computing clinics
- Blended e-learning for children

### Success Metrics Defined
- Number of logins (engagement)
- Hours on e-library (adoption)
- Primary school completion rates (outcome)

### Risk Analysis
Government failure, tech rejection, fixed mindsets, poor reception to technology.

### What I Learned
Real solutions need government buy-in. Start with one community, not all of Lagos. Measure attempts (logins), not just outcomes.
